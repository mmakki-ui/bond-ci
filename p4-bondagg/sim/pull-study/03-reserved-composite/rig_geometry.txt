VERDICT
================================================================================================================
  OPEN AND REPORTED, NOT GUESSED: nothing here measures the TRUE distribution of
  stall count, duration and position on a real tether.  The ensemble prior is
  uniform over what the rig can represent, which is a statement about the rig,
  not about the world.  Only ADR-004 condition 2 -- a hardware trace -- can
  supply the real one.  Until then the ensemble is a SENSITIVITY, and the honest
  reading of any absolute-loss number is "canonical value +- the geometry spread
  printed above", not the canonical value alone.
  Reported as measured.  No bar was widened, no scheduler was tuned, and no file
  outside this one was edited.
